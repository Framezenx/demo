SCHWARZSCHILD RADIUS

10 M☉

Event-horizon radius of a non-rotating black hole.

Rs =
29.5 km

Rs = 2GM / c² ≈ 2.95 km per solar mass.
Assumes zero spin and no charge.

The event horizon is the boundary beyond which nothing — not even light — can escape. Around it, an accretion disk of superheated matter glows brightly. Real black holes bend this light around themselves (gravitational lensing).

Leaves out: spin, charge, relativistic ray-tracing, Doppler beaming, real observational data.

Wormhole Explorer

A theoretical concept, not observed, not proven. This is a stylized illustration of curved spacetime — not a real travel system or technology.

HYPOTHETICAL

Wormhole

A wormhole is a hypothetical shortcut through spacetime connecting two distant regions. General relativity allows such solutions mathematically, but no wormhole has ever been observed.

Traversable wormholes would require exotic matter with negative energy density — something never observed. Everything you see here is a stylized visual metaphor.

ABOUT THIS BUILD

COSMIC INFINITY v2.0 — The Universe Is Waiting. A single-file, offline-friendly educational space explorer built with plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Three.js.

What is real: planetary diameters, masses, orbital periods and distances from NASA fact sheets; the Schwarzschild radius formula; the encyclopedia facts; confirmed exoplanet data.

What is simplified: all 3D distances, sizes and orbital speeds are compressed for display. The black-hole and wormhole images are illustrations. The star map's positions are decorative.

What is not here: live API feeds. No astronomy data is fetched at runtime, so nothing can be mislabelled as "live".
